Working definitions

Terms used by the proposal

Integration
A reciprocal process involving participation, access, communication and adaptation; not a claim that lawful cultural difference must disappear.
Standard
A proposed expectation or quality benchmark. It is not legally compulsory unless a valid legal basis makes it so.
Authority
Power derived from an identified lawful role, not status assumed through confidence, branding or informal influence.
Compliance
Action consistent with an applicable lawful requirement. Barriers, misunderstanding and inability must not automatically be treated as deliberate refusal.
Safeguarding
Action intended to protect people from abuse, neglect, exploitation or avoidable harm through lawful and accountable processes.
Participation
Engagement with an offered or required process, subject to its legal basis, accessibility, informed understanding and relevant rights.
