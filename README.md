# ASU Immigration Proposed Standards

Provider-neutral static website for the **ASU Immigration Proposed Standards Theory**, an independent ongoing investigation by **Toby William Duffield**.

## Correct domain

`asu-immigration-proposed-standards.uk`

The supplied `CNAME`, canonical URLs, Open Graph URLs, sitemap and robots file all use this domain.

## How the website connects the existing projects

This version explicitly joins:

1. **ASU Ltd — Intergenerational Goal Continuity and Social Evolution Theory**  
   https://asultd.com/

2. **Ace Scaffolding UK — Standards, Access & Communication Hub**  
   https://acescaffolding.uk/

3. **ASU Immigration Proposed Standards**  
   https://asu-immigration-proposed-standards.uk/

The connection is:

- `asultd.com` supplies the proposed explanation of how individual psychology, family influence, social environments and historical change may shape behaviour and expectations.
- `acescaffolding.uk` supplies the visible standards and communication structure: clarity, authority, suitable channels, accountability and lawful conduct.
- This website applies both ideas to immigration through a reciprocal standards and integration pathway.

## Public wording decision

The private message screenshots were used as concept-origin notes but are not included in the public site package.

The website preserves the underlying questions concerning:

- clear standards and expectations;
- integration and language;
- lawful employment and participation;
- public protection;
- deterrence and compliance;
- consequences through the correct legal process.

It does **not** reproduce or adopt derogatory wording, racial generalisations, forced-labour ideas, detention disguised as rehabilitation, collective punishment or automatic deportation for programme non-participation.

## Essential review before political or operational use

This package is not legal advice and does not establish that the proposed model is lawful or effective. Before presenting it as a practical policy, commission review by:

1. a UK immigration and public-law solicitor;
2. an equality and human-rights specialist;
3. an employment and modern-slavery specialist;
4. safeguarding and trafficking practitioners;
5. migrant, refugee and local-community representatives;
6. a policy evaluator or academic researcher.
