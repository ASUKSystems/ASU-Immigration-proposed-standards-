Pre-implementation assessment

Questions that must be answered

Legal boundary

Equality

Could a requirement create direct or indirect discrimination, and is any differential treatment objectively and lawfully justified?

Legal boundary

Human rights

Could liberty, family life, privacy, expression, religion, education, work or access to remedy be affected?

Proposal control

Vulnerability

How will disability, trauma, age, language, health, exploitation and caring responsibilities be identified and accommodated?

Open question

Unintended effects

Could the proposal increase fear, avoidance of services, destitution, exploitation, community tension or administrative delay?
